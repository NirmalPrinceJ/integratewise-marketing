## Section 2 — Problem

### Heading

# Your tools are connected. Your work is not.

### Body

Customer context lives in CRM. Decisions live in chat. Commitments live in email. Delivery context lives in project tools. Knowledge lives in documents and in people’s heads.

Every handoff loses part of the story. Every new person reconstructs it. Every AI session starts with another brief.

The problem is no longer access to software. It is continuity between systems, people, decisions, and action.

### Three problem statements

#### Context fragments

The same account, project, or decision appears differently across every tool. No surface carries the complete operational picture.

#### Handoffs reset work

When ownership changes, the new owner inherits records but not the reasoning, commitments, evidence, or unresolved questions behind them.

#### AI starts incomplete

Models can be capable and still produce weak work when they lack governed, current, organisation-specific context.

## Section 3 — Category explanation

### Heading

# Connection moves data. Continuity keeps work moving.

### Body

Integrations can transfer records between applications. Continuity preserves what those records mean, how they relate, what changed, who decided, what is pending, and what should happen next.

IntegrateWise creates that continuity through one workspace, one trust boundary, and one operational Spine.

| Connection | Continuity |
|---|---|
| Moves data between tools | Preserves operational meaning across tools |
| Synchronizes fields | Connects entities, evidence, decisions, and timelines |

### Body

A single Ecosystem Connection establishes the workspace trust boundary. The Integration Manager enables approved systems inside it. IntegrateWise loads, normalizes, and stores their operational context in the Spine, then projects that context into the right workbench for each role.

## Platform model

### Heading

# Stable truth underneath. Adaptive work above.

### Body

The platform separates what must remain deterministic from what can adapt.

The Spine protects canonical operational truth. Workbenches adapt that truth to each role. The Twin reasons over context. Governance controls authority. The capability layer executes approved operations. Reconciliation closes the loop.

---

## Architecture layers

### Ecosystem Connection

One authenticated workspace boundary for approved organisational systems and capabilities.

### Integration Manager

Configures providers, scopes, adapter type, sync policy, health, write permissions, and approval requirements.

### Spine Ingestion

Loads raw source data, normalizes it deterministically, resolves external identities, and writes governed canonical state.

### Spine and Entity360

Maintains entities, relationships, timelines, evidence, versions, and the context required to understand operational state.

### Projection Engine

Renders role-specific workbenches from shared canonical context without creating competing systems of truth.

### Twin

Uses Entity360 context to orient, analyze, draft, compare, and propose bounded next actions.

### Approval Center

Evaluates actor, operation, policy, scopes, side effects, and risk before execution.

### Capability Fabric

Routes approved operations to the correct provider capability without exposing provider credentials to the workbench or model.

### Reconciliation

Re-ingests execution results, verifies outcomes, resolves drift, and rehydrates the workspace from confirmed state.

---

## Platform principles

### One tenant, one Spine

Every workspace has a clear trust and data boundary. Projections share canonical context without mixing tenant state.

### Frontends read from the Spine

Workbench surfaces do not reconstruct truth independently from provider APIs.

### AI proposes, governed services write

Models do not mutate canonical operational state directly.

### Credentials stay behind capability boundaries

Presentation, reasoning, governance, and provider execution remain separated.

### Every write returns through reconciliation

A successful API response is not treated as operational truth until the result is verified and re-ingested.
